Our Work
Real Projects. Real Results.

Every image below is from an actual job done by our team. No stock photos, no filler.

Terrace Waterproofing

Torch-Applied Bituminous Sheet

Heat-fused bituminous membrane being laid on a rooftop — creates a seamless, impermeable barrier that bonds directly to the substrate.

Basement / Below-Grade

HDPE Waterproof Membrane

High-density polyethylene sheets staged for installation — the preferred choice for below-ground slabs and basement walls requiring long-term protection.

Terrace Waterproofing

Brick Bat Coba Method

Traditional brick bat coba work on a residential terrace — the brick layer is laid over a waterproof membrane bed, protecting the slab against standing water and thermal expansion.

Bathroom Waterproofing

Floor Treatment — Completed

Clean, treated bathroom floor post-waterproofing. Cementitious coating penetrates deep into the substrate — not just a surface layer that will eventually crack.

Quality Verification

24-Hour Water Ponding Test

We always flood-test the treated surface before signing off. Water is retained at depth for 24 hours — if the ceiling below stays dry, the job is done right.

Underground Tank

UG Tank — Completed & Tested

Large underground tank fully coated with liquid waterproofing membrane. The green coating confirms complete, even coverage — followed by a water fill test before handover.

Team at Work

Liquid Membrane Application

Our crew applying liquid waterproofing coating on a terrace project — full protective gear, uniform application technique, and no shortcuts on overlap or edge sealing.

Balcony / Slab

Cementitious Slurry Coating

Balcony slab treated with crystalline cementitious slurry — the compound reacts with moisture inside the concrete to form permanent waterproof crystals, not just a surface film.

Large-Scale Project

Full Terrace Overhaul

Old waterproofing stripped, substrate prepared, and fresh brick bat coba layer being laid on a large residential terrace — the kind of thorough job other contractors skip.
